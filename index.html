01 Statement

She works with the most common materials in the world. Paper. Found things. Whatever is within reach. The material is never the point. The *vision* is. With *intention* and *skill*, ordinary matter becomes a *place* you can walk into.

02 The difference

Everyone has the scraps.
Only she sees the picture.

Her sculptures hold still the instant a person decides to change. Not a picture of that moment, but the moment itself.

  1. ScrapsOld newspaper. Anyone could pick it up.

03 The material

Paper had limits.
So she made her own.

Three years of testing led to her own fiber medium, light enough to hang large forms on invisible strings.

  1. Raw fiberShort fibers, loose and light.
  2. LayeredPressed on in soft, wet layers.
  3. SandedSmoothed until the color comes through.
  4. FinishedFull color. A wave caught as it breaks.
A wave, held still.
