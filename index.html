01 Statement

She works with the most common materials in the world. Paper. Found things. Whatever is within reach. The material is never the point. The *vision* is. With *intention* and *skill*, ordinary matter becomes a *place* you can walk into.

02 The difference

Everyone has the scraps.
Only she sees the picture.

Her sculptures hold still the instant a person decides to change. Not a picture of that moment, but the moment itself, suspended so you can step inside.

It starts with paper. Over three years she developed her own fiber-based medium, light enough to hang large forms on invisible strings.

Here, scraps of old newspaper. Press and hold to add the one thing nobody else has.

03 The material

From paper to her own material.

One thread runs through all of it. Drag the knot along and watch each step come in.

Drag the knot →
Where it began

Paper.

Newspaper anyone could find. Torn, layered and shaped into figures that float.

Three years of testing

Paper had limits.

So she studied every medium she could and kept what each does best: the give of clay, the strength of wood and steel, the lightness of wax.

Now

Her own material.

A fiber medium she invented. It can be sculpted, sanded and painted, yet stays light enough to hang on invisible strings.

In the studio now

A wave, held still.

Water caught the instant it breaks: the thing that gives life and can overwhelm it. Stingrays rise through it. One moment of change, held open for you to stand in.
